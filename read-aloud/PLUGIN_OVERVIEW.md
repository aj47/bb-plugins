Listen to your agents instead of reading them. Turn on Read Aloud in a thread
and each reply is spoken as it arrives, so you can reply by typing or dictating
without looking at the screen.

## What you get

- A speaker button beside the composer in every thread. Tap it to turn read
  aloud on or off; tap while it's talking to stop the current message.
- A Read aloud button on every message that reads just that message.
- "Read replies aloud" in each thread's actions menu.
- "Read last reply aloud" in the composer's + menu.
- Settings for every message vs final message only, speaking speed and voice.

## How it works

Speech uses your device's built-in voices, so nothing is sent to an external
service. On Android it needs the bb voice app, version 0.4.0 or later. Audio plays on the device that has the thread open and
in front, so your desktop stays quiet while you listen on your phone. Tapping
any button, including the mic, stops playback so dictation doesn't record it.
The setting stays on per thread until you turn it off.
